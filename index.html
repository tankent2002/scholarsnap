    Checked carefully — including the corrections

    This list was verified entry by entry against official provider pages on 13 Nov 2026. That checking caught real errors in widely-circulated listings, which is why this page states them plainly:

    • A*STAR: there is no “A*STAR Undergraduate Scholarship” on A*STAR's current page. The real undergraduate award is the National Science Scholarship (BS–PhD), closing 1 March — and no S$55,000/year figure is published.
    • SMU Merit Scholarship: the S$12,000 Growth Opportunities Allowance is a total for the degree, not per year. The award is a full four-year tuition waiver plus that allowance, bond-free.
    • MOE bursaries: there is no “University Admission Bursary”. The real Higher Education Bursary is S$3,250 or S$1,350 a year (AY2026), with the income threshold now at per-capita income ≤ S$3,000.
    • SG Enable: no “SG Enable Post-Secondary Scholarship” could be found on any official page, so it is not listed. The verified disability-specific award here is the APB Foundation Scholarship (via SPD), S$15,000 a year.
    • NTU Science & Technology Scholarship: verified as a real award — but it is for Asian citizens except Singapore, so it is deliberately not in this list for Singapore Citizens.
    • Where details are thin (MAS, IMDA SG Digital, NLB, JTC, NParks, HPB, CAAS): the card says so, and marks which figures come from secondary listings. Re-check those on the provider page before applying.
